## Purpose

AO Knowledge Base is the permanent business knowledge base for AI Operator OS.

It captures locked strategy, operating principles, architecture direction, financial rules, business evaluation logic, and durable decisions that should guide future development and business execution.

## AI Operator OS Identity

AI Operator OS is an AI-native Business Operating System designed to help one CEO build, operate, optimize, and scale a portfolio of businesses from a single command center.

It is built first for Jake as CEO. If it makes the CEO successful, it may later become a product for others.

## Core Purpose

AI Operator OS should continuously build, operate, optimize, and scale businesses while keeping the CEO's operational workload as close to constant as possible.

## Success Definition

Short term, AI Operator OS should become self-sustaining and stop requiring out-of-pocket funding.

Long term, it should replace dependence on employment income and support financial independence.

## Documentation Philosophy

The AO Knowledge Base is the permanent source of truth for AI Operator OS.

Major architectural decisions should be documented before implementation whenever practical.

The codebase should reflect the knowledge contained within this documentation.

If documentation and implementation ever disagree, the documentation should be reviewed before continuing development.

## Core Principles

- CEO time is sacred.
- The company must be self-sustaining before owner withdrawals.
- Lean first, scale second.
- Use money intelligently and reinvest where it creates the best long-term return.
- The system should never simply run; it should continuously improve.
- Architecture comes before UI.
- Progressive complexity: only add complexity when it creates immediate value.
- Businesses must match the CEO's current stage, time availability, and budget.
- Bottleneck elimination matters more than flashy automation.
- AI models and tools should be selected by ROI, not hype.
- The system must stay organized and easy to understand as it grows.

## Operating Model

- CEO sets direction, approves risk, completes unavoidable human actions, and reviews performance.
- Executive Coordinator manages company operations.
- Departments own major business functions.
- Departments scale through squads.
- Operators perform specialized tasks.
- Work should flow through structured records and workflows, not random chat messages.

## Financial Model

- Current stage: investment stage.
- First target: AO OS pays for its own operating costs.
- Next target: AO OS funds its own growth.
- Later target: AO OS replaces employment income.
- Owner withdrawals come after operating costs and growth needs are covered.

## Business Strategy

AI Operator OS should start with businesses requiring the least CEO time.

Higher-touch businesses, such as website sales requiring calls, may be enabled later when CEO availability increases.

Every business idea should be scored by profit potential, automation readiness, required CEO time, startup cost, and fit with current stage.

The right business at the wrong time is the wrong business.

## Architecture Direction

AI Operator OS is not one automation platform.

AI Operator OS manages multiple automation platforms and business blueprints. It should be able to house future AI-powered businesses that do not exist yet and expand without requiring a redesign.
