# Decision-0013 - Continuous Business Operations

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-30

## Purpose

Define whether businesses inside AI Operator OS should depend on the CEO being actively present.

## Decision

Businesses inside AI Operator OS should continue operating independent of the CEO's schedule. The CEO provides direction, approvals, and unavoidable human actions, but the businesses should keep running while the CEO is asleep, working, or away.

## Reasoning

AI Operator OS is intended to behave like a company, not a chatbot. A real company does not stop operating when the CEO is not present.

## Future Considerations

The system should eventually support background operations, alerts, morning briefs, approval queues, and autonomous work within approved limits.
