# Decision-0010 - Model and Tool ROI

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Define how AI models and tools should be selected.

## Decision

AI models and tools should be selected by ROI, not hype.

## Reasoning

AI Operator OS should use money intelligently. Models, tools, and services should be chosen because they improve outcomes, reduce bottlenecks, or create long-term return.

## Future Considerations

Future tool evaluation should consider cost, reliability, business impact, automation readiness, and fit with the CEO's current stage.
