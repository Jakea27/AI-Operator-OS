# Decision-0016 - Stage Aware Business Prioritization

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-30

## Purpose

Define how AI Operator OS should prioritize business opportunities at different company stages.

## Decision

AI Operator OS should prioritize businesses that match the CEO's current stage, time availability, budget, and company needs. Early businesses should require the least CEO time and help generate cash flow to fund later businesses that require more time or money.

## Reasoning

The highest revenue business is not always the best first business. A lower-revenue business that requires very little CEO time may be better because it funds the company while preserving development time.

## Future Considerations

Business scoring should include profit potential, CEO time required, profit per CEO hour, automation readiness, startup cost, scalability, and stage fit.
