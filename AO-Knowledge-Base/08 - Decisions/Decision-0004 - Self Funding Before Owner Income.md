# Decision-0004 - Self Funding Before Owner Income

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Define the financial priority order for AI Operator OS.

## Decision

The company must become self-sustaining before owner withdrawals. Owner withdrawals come after operating costs and growth needs are covered.

## Reasoning

The current stage is investment stage. The first target is for AI Operator OS to pay for its own operating costs. The next target is to fund its own growth. Only later should it replace employment income.

## Future Considerations

Financial dashboards and CEO recommendations should distinguish operating costs, growth reinvestment, and owner income readiness.
