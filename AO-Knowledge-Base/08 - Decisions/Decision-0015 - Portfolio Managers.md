# Decision-0015 - Portfolio Managers

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-30

## Purpose

Define how multiple businesses should be grouped and managed as AI Operator OS scales.

## Decision

Businesses should be grouped by type under Portfolio Managers. For example, content businesses should be managed under a Content Portfolio Manager, ecommerce businesses under an Ecommerce Portfolio Manager, and service businesses under a Service Portfolio Manager.

## Reasoning

As businesses scale, the CEO should not directly manage every individual business. Portfolio Managers organize similar businesses, compare performance, share knowledge, and prevent the company from becoming chaotic.

## Future Considerations

Portfolio Managers should report to the Executive Coordinator. They should optimize groups of businesses, not micromanage every operator.
