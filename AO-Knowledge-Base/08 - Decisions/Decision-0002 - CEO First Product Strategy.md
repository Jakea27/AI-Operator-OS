# Decision-0002 - CEO First Product Strategy

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Define who AI Operator OS is built for first.

## Decision

AI Operator OS is built first for Jake as CEO. If it makes the CEO successful, it may later become a product for others.

## Reasoning

The first priority is solving the real operating needs of the CEO. A system that creates measurable success for its first user has a stronger foundation for becoming a broader product later.

## Future Considerations

If the system becomes useful to others, productization should happen after the internal operating system proves value.
