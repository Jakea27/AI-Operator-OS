# Decision-0007 - Departments Scale Through Squads

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Define how the operating model should scale.

## Decision

Departments own major business functions. Departments scale through squads. Operators perform specialized tasks.

## Reasoning

This structure allows the Executive Coordinator to manage company operations while keeping responsibilities organized. It also gives AI Operator OS room to grow without becoming a collection of disconnected assistants.

## Future Considerations

Future department and squad design should preserve clear ownership, shared records, and CEO approval boundaries.
