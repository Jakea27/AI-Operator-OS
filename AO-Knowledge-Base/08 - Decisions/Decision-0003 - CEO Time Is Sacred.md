# Decision-0003 - CEO Time Is Sacred

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Establish CEO time as a primary design constraint.

## Decision

CEO time is sacred. AI Operator OS should continuously build, operate, optimize, and scale businesses while keeping the CEO's operational workload as close to constant as possible.

## Reasoning

The system exists to increase business capacity without proportionally increasing CEO workload. If growth requires too much additional CEO time, the system is failing one of its core purposes.

## Future Considerations

Business ideas, workflows, and automations should be evaluated by how much CEO time they require.
