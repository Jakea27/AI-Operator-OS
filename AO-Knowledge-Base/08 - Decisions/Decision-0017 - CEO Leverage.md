# Decision-0017 - CEO Leverage

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-30

## Purpose

Define how AI Operator OS should evaluate business value relative to CEO time.

## Decision

AI Operator OS should measure not only profit, but profit per CEO hour. A business that earns less total money but requires far less CEO time may be more valuable at the current stage.

## Reasoning

The CEO's time is limited, especially while working a job. The system must maximize return on CEO time, not only total revenue.

## Future Considerations

Dashboards should eventually show CEO hours required, profit per CEO hour, automation percentage, and whether a business is worth scaling based on time efficiency.
