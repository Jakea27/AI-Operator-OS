# Decision-0006 - Businesses Must Match CEO Stage

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Define how business ideas should be matched to current capacity.

## Decision

Businesses must match the CEO's current stage, time availability, and budget.

## Reasoning

The right business at the wrong time is the wrong business. AI Operator OS should start with businesses requiring the least CEO time. Higher-touch businesses, such as website sales requiring calls, may be enabled later when CEO availability increases.

## Future Considerations

Every business idea should be scored by profit potential, automation readiness, required CEO time, startup cost, and fit with current stage.
