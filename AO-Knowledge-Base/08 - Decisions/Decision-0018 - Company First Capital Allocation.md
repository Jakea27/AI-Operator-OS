# Decision-0018 - Company First Capital Allocation

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-30

## Purpose

Define how AI Operator OS should prioritize the use of company-generated money.

## Decision

Money generated by AI Operator OS should first keep the company operating, then fund growth, then build reserves, and only later support owner withdrawals when doing so does not weaken company growth.

## Reasoning

If the CEO's job covers personal bills, AO OS can retain more cash to improve itself, launch businesses, buy tools, and compound faster. Early withdrawals can slow the company's long-term growth.

## Future Considerations

The Finance Department should eventually recommend reinvestment, reserves, AI tool upgrades, new business funding, and owner distributions based on company stage and financial health.
