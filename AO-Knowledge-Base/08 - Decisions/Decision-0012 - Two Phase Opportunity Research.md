# Decision-0012 - Two Phase Opportunity Research

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-30

## Purpose

Define how new business ideas should be researched before deeper resources are committed.

## Decision

Every new business idea should go through two research phases.

Phase 1 is Opportunity Validation. It should be broad, fast, and low-cost. Its purpose is to score the idea before deeper resources are spent.

Phase 2 is Business Blueprint Research. It only happens after CEO approval. Its purpose is to deeply research the approved opportunity and prepare it for launch.

## Reasoning

AI Operator OS must save time and money while still producing high-quality decisions. It should not deeply research every idea before knowing whether the opportunity is worth pursuing.

## Future Considerations

Phase 1 should score profit potential, automation readiness, startup cost, CEO time required, risk, scalability, and fit with the CEO's current stage. Phase 2 should produce the business blueprint, launch plan, budget, workflows, required tools, KPIs, and department assignments.
