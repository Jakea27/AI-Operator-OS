# Decision-0001 - AI Operator OS Identity

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

Define what AI Operator OS is at the highest level.

## Decision

AI Operator OS is an AI-native Business Operating System designed to help one CEO build, operate, optimize, and scale a portfolio of businesses from a single command center.

## Reasoning

The system should not be limited to a single business, automation, or tool. It needs to coordinate business creation, operations, optimization, and scaling across multiple future opportunities.

## Future Considerations

Future modules should support new business types without requiring a redesign of the core operating system.
