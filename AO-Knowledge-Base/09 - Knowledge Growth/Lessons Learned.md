## Purpose

This file will collect durable lessons learned while building and operating AI Operator OS and its businesses.

Lessons should focus on what happened, why it happened, what was learned, and what should be done differently in the future.

## Initial Guidance

- Keep lessons specific and actionable.
- Link lessons to related decisions when possible.
- Record both failures and wins.
- Prefer useful truth over polished hindsight.
