## Purpose

This file will collect repeatable practices that improve business operations, development, research, marketing, finance, automation, and CEO leverage.

Best practices should evolve from repeated lessons, validated experiments, and observed operational wins.

## Initial Guidance

- Add practices only when they are useful across more than one situation.
- Keep each practice clear enough for future operators to follow.
- Revisit best practices when new evidence contradicts them.
