## Purpose

This file will track experiments run by AI Operator OS and its businesses.

Experiments may include business tests, marketing tests, AI workflow tests, automation tests, pricing tests, product tests, and operational process tests.

## Initial Guidance

- Define the hypothesis before running an experiment.
- Record the result honestly.
- Capture what should change because of the result.
- Separate failed experiments from bad execution when possible.
