## Purpose

This file will collect insights that improve future business selection, operation, optimization, and scaling.

Insights may come from customer behavior, market research, financial performance, operational bottlenecks, AI tool usage, or failed and successful launches.

## Initial Guidance

- Connect insights to business decisions when possible.
- Keep insights practical and grounded in evidence.
- Prefer insights that improve future execution.
