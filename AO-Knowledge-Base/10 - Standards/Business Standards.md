# Business Standards

Status: Draft  
Version: 0.1  
Owner: Jake Allen  
Last Updated: 2026-06-29

## Purpose

This file will define standards for evaluating, launching, operating, and scaling businesses inside AI Operator OS.

Business standards should protect CEO time, capital, and focus.

## Initial Direction

- Businesses must match the CEO's current stage, time availability, and budget.
- Score business ideas by profit potential, automation readiness, required CEO time, startup cost, and stage fit.
- Prefer lean validation before scale.
- Avoid high-touch businesses until CEO availability supports them.
- Reinvest where it creates the best long-term return.

## Future Evolution

This file should eventually include business scoring rubrics, launch requirements, operating benchmarks, and shutdown criteria.
