# AI Operator OS Vision

Version: 0.1.0
Owner: Jake Allen
Source of Truth: GitHub
Working Office: ChatGPT Project
Last Updated: 2026-06-23

## North Star

AI Operator OS exists to maximize owner profit while minimizing owner time.

## Mission

Build a business operating system that helps a single owner discover opportunities, make better decisions, automate repetitive work, and maximize profit through AI-assisted workflows while maintaining complete human control.

## Core Principles

1. Profit over complexity.
2. Human approval for all high-impact actions.
3. Local-first until cloud creates positive ROI.
4. Minimize recurring costs.
5. Maintain one permanent evolving codebase.
6. Every feature must satisfy at least one:
   - Increase revenue
   - Save time
   - Improve decision quality
   - Automate repetitive work
7. Build for long-term maintainability, not short-term demos.

## Philosophy

AI Operator OS is not designed to replace the owner. It is designed to multiply the owner's effectiveness.

AI prepares, recommends, drafts, analyzes, and automates repetitive work. The owner approves direction.

## Long-Term Vision

AI Operator OS becomes the central operating system for every business the owner creates.

It coordinates:

- Business strategy
- Research
- Development
- Sales
- Delivery
- Analytics
- Knowledge management

The owner remains CEO. AI acts as specialized departments that continuously produce recommendations, draft work, analyze results, and execute approved workflows.

## Success Metrics

### Financial

- Monthly revenue
- Monthly profit
- Operating cost
- Profit margin

### Automation

- Hours saved
- Tasks automated
- Approvals completed

### Business

- Leads generated
- Clients acquired
- Projects delivered
- Owner time required
- System health

## Constraints

- No feature bloat
- No unnecessary subscriptions
- No duplicate systems
- No multiple codebases
- No automation without approval for critical actions
- No feature may increase monthly operating costs unless expected ROI is positive
