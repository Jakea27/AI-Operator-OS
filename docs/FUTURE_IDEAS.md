# Future Ideas

## Purpose

Store ideas that are intentionally not on the current roadmap.

This document keeps promising concepts visible without letting them distract from the active operating system milestones.

## Idea Format

Every idea should include:

- Title
- Description
- Potential Value
- Complexity
- Priority
- Status: Idea, Research, or Planned

## Business Ideas

### AI Operator OS Templates Marketplace

- Title: AI Operator OS Templates Marketplace
- Description: Create reusable business, department, SOP, and operator templates that can be imported into local workspaces.
- Potential Value: Speeds up setup for new businesses and creates a future distribution path.
- Complexity: High
- Priority: Medium
- Status: Idea

### Local Business Health Score

- Title: Local Business Health Score

- Priority: Medium
