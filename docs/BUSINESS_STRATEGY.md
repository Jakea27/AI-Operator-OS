# Business Strategy

Version: 0.1.0
Last Updated: 2026-07-18

## First Business Goal

Use AI Operator OS to help generate the first $100 in revenue before adding paid infrastructure.

## Preferred Early Revenue Models

- AI lead generation service
- Local business website audit
- AI automation setup
- Short-form content repurposing
- Resume and job application assistant

## Early Rule

Manual approval mode until the system has helped generate at least $1,000.

## Execution and AI Strategy

AI Operator OS separates execution infrastructure from AI intelligence.

AO-012 focuses on proving that the operating system can execute deterministic work end-to-end without AI providers. This keeps the foundation testable, auditable, and safe before intelligence is layered in.

AO-013 introduces AI Provider Integration after the execution workflow is stable. Departments and workers request capabilities; the Provider Manager selects providers based on capability, cost, speed, availability, and business rules.

This protects the business from vendor lock-in and keeps provider selection tied to ROI rather than hype.
