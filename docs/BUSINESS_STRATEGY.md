## First Business Goal

Use AI Operator OS to help generate the first $100 in revenue before adding paid infrastructure.

- AI lead generation service
- Local business website audit
- AI automation setup
- Short-form content repurposing
- Resume and job application assistant

## Early Rule

Manual approval mode until the system has helped generate at least $1,000.
